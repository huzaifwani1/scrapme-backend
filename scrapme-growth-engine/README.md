# ScrapMe Growth Engine

Internal customer growth, CRM, marketing automation and analytics platform for ScrapMe.

## Core Systems

- Customer CRM
- Email marketing
- WhatsApp automation
- Customer segmentation
- Campaign management
- Marketing attribution
- Google Analytics integration
- Google Search Console integration
- Instagram/influencer attribution
- Order-based automations
- Retention automation
- Referral campaigns
- Growth analytics

## Principle

ScrapMe application = transaction engine.

Growth Engine = customer, marketing, retention and automation engine.

The Growth Engine must not directly modify production business logic without explicit integration.
